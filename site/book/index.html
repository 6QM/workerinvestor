Preface / excerptPreface / excerpt

工资之外，不是工作之外。Beyond wages is not beyond work.

我也还在工作系统里。工资给我安全感，也提醒我边界：如果未来只能继续靠同一份时间出售自己，我拥有的选择权就会很少。I am still inside the work system too. Wages give me safety, and also remind me of the boundary: if the future can only come from selling the same block of time, I own very little optionality.

The Worker Investor is not someone who stops working. It is someone who stops treating work as the only place where the future can be built.
Human Capital ETF
Core生存和复利的底仓The base for survival and compounding

健康、现金流、职业主线、工程能力和底层学习能力。Health, cash flow, career line, engineering ability, and foundational learning.

Growth面向未来的新能力New abilities aimed at the future

AI、代码、英语、阅读、写作，以及能够放大主业的新技能。AI, code, English, reading, writing, and skills that amplify the core work.

Distribution把私人经验变成公开资产Turn private experience into public assets

文章、网站、视频、newsletter、作品集和可以被市场发现的表达。Articles, websites, videos, newsletters, portfolios, and output that can be discovered by the market.

Meta再平衡系统The rebalancing system

时间分配、风险控制、复盘、注意力管理和长期方向。Time allocation, risk control, review, attention management, and long-term direction.
