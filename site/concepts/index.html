Concept glossaryConcept glossary Wages -> Ownership -> Optionality

先把词定义清楚，写作才不会漂。Define the terms clearly so the writing does not drift.

这些不是花哨概念，而是整本书和网站会反复使用的工作语言。These are not decorative terms. They are the working language that the book and site will keep using.

OPERATING SYSTEM Worker -> Investor
  1. 01 Wages
  2. 02 Allocation
  3. 03 Assets
  4. 04 Optionality
Worker

依赖工资和组织系统生活的人。这里不是身份贬低，而是现实起点。A person who lives through wages and organizational systems. This is not an insult; it is the starting condition.

Investor

把资源转化为未来资产的人，不只是买卖股票的人。Someone who turns resources into future assets, not merely someone who trades stocks.

Ownership

对资产、系统、判断、内容和未来选择权拥有一部分控制。Partial control over assets, systems, judgment, content, and future optionality.

Optionality

未来不被单一路径锁死的能力。它可能来自现金、技能、声誉、健康、内容和关系。The ability not to be locked into a single future path. It can come from cash, skills, reputation, health, content, and relationships.

Human Capital ETF

把自己看成一个需要配置、复盘和再平衡的长期组合。A way to treat the self as a long-term portfolio that needs allocation, review, and rebalancing.

Distribution

把私人经验变成可被发现、分享、引用和持续工作的公开资产。Turning private experience into public assets that can be discovered, shared, cited, and keep working.

The Worker Investor

书是主干，网站是长期研究库。The book is the trunk. The site is the long-running research library.
