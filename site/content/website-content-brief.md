# The Worker Investor Website Content Brief

This document is the content source of truth for the current website direction.

## One-Sentence Positioning

The Worker Investor is a modern nonfiction book and living research project about how an ordinary worker can turn wages, skills, AI, writing, and investing into long-term assets and optionality.

## Core Lines

- 工资结算劳动，资产保存劳动。
- 工资之外，不是工作之外，而是单一路径之外。
- Worker gets cash flow. Investor converts cash flow into ownership, optionality, and future time.
- Not escaping work, but building optionality inside and beyond work.

## Reader

The primary reader is an ordinary worker, engineer, office worker, or knowledge worker who still depends on wages but wants a second curve.

They may feel:

- wages keep me safe, but also define my boundary;
- my time is being sold too cheaply;
- I want to invest, but money is not the only asset;
- I want AI, writing, skills, and projects to become part of my future;
- I want choice, not just productivity.

## Site Promise

The website should prove four things quickly:

1. This is a real book project.
2. The author is still building it, honestly and visibly.
3. The project has a method, not just a slogan.
4. The site will keep growing through posts, tools, appendices, and case studies.

## Tone

The website should sound like a working author building a serious book, not a platform selling a dream.

Prefer:

- first-person when honest;
- concrete claims;
- visible uncertainty;
- clear terms;
- short paragraphs;
- plain language.

Avoid:

- fake authority;
- fake social proof;
- startup hype;
- financial freedom promises;
- AI-generated filler;
- slogans that do not explain anything.

## Maintenance Rule

New content should enter one of four places:

- stable argument: book;
- detailed supplement: appendix;
- fresh observation: library post;
- practical action material: resources.

If the idea is still unclear, keep it private.
