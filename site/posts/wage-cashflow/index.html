工资现金流如何进入资产系统How wage cash flow enters an asset system

把发薪日画成一张流量图：生活成本、学习投入、金融资产和内容系统分别吃掉了多少现金流。A payday flow chart: living cost, learning, financial assets, and content systems each take a share of cash flow.

Field note

Wages -> Allocation -> Assets -> Optionality

工资不是羞耻，也不是终局。它是第一个可配置的现金流。真正的问题不是今天是否还需要工资，而是每一次发薪以后，有多少比例被消费掉，有多少比例变成了未来仍然能工作的东西。Wages are neither shameful nor final. They are the first cash flow that can be allocated. The real question is not whether I still need wages today, but what share of each payday becomes something that can keep working in the future.

我会把它拆成四个出口：生活成本保护 Core，学习和工具扩大 Growth，公开表达进入 Distribution，复盘和风险控制进入 Meta。这个分配不需要一开始完美，但需要被看见。I split it into four exits: living costs protect Core, learning and tools expand Growth, public output enters Distribution, and review plus risk control enters Meta. The allocation does not need to be perfect at first, but it needs to be visible.

这就是这个网站存在的原因之一：把原本散落在账单、笔记、项目和阅读里的东西，变成一个可以被复盘、被搜索、被继续建设的系统。That is one reason this site exists: to turn what is scattered across bills, notes, projects, and reading into a system that can be reviewed, searched, and continued.

书是主干，网站是长期研究库。The book is the trunk. The site is the long-running research library.
