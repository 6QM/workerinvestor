Site architectureSite architecture

这不是一页展示，而是一套可维护的出版系统。This is no longer a one-page showcase. It is a maintainable publishing system.

我把优秀作者网站的做法压缩成四层：书承载主干，附录承载复杂材料，文章承载新观察，后台保留未成熟素材。I compressed the patterns of strong author sites into four layers: the book carries the spine, the appendix carries complex material, posts carry fresh observations, and private notes keep immature material backstage.

Canonical Book

稳定判断进入书。Stable judgment enters the book.

能经得住几年、代表核心主线的内容，才进入正式章节。书要克制、清晰、可反复读。Only ideas that can hold up for years and carry the main argument enter formal chapters. The book should stay restrained, clear, and rereadable.

01
Living Appendix

复杂材料放进活附录。Complex material belongs in the living appendix.

图表、模型、术语解释、章节补充、勘误和版本更新，可以持续扩展，不挤压正文。Charts, models, glossary notes, chapter supplements, corrections, and version notes can keep expanding without crowding the manuscript.

02
Field Notes

新想法先发成 post。New ideas begin as posts.

案例、数据、读书笔记、AI 观察和作者实验，先作为公开笔记积累时间戳和搜索入口。Cases, data, reading notes, AI observations, and personal experiments can first become public notes with timestamps and search surface.

03
Private Draft

还没想清楚的留在后台。Unclear thoughts stay backstage.

原始素材、未成熟判断和私人复盘，不急着公开。网站只承载已经能帮助读者的版本。Raw notes, immature judgment, and private reviews do not need to be public. The site carries the version that can help a reader.
