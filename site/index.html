Worker Investor principlesWorker Investor principles

四条原则给这个项目定边界。Four principles set the boundary for this project.

01

工资是入口，不是终点。Wages are the entrance, not the destination.

工资先让人活下来，然后才有机会被转化为本金、技能、内容和系统。Wages keep a person alive first. Then they can become principal, skills, content, and systems.

02

Exchange 必要，但有边界。Exchange is necessary, but bounded.

用时间交换工资是起点；问题在于未来是否仍然只能继续交换。Trading time for wages is a starting point. The question is whether the future can be built beyond that trade.

03

Ownership 是第二身份。Ownership is a second identity.

Investor 不是炒股的人，而是把资源变成未来资产的人。An investor is not merely someone who trades stocks. It is someone who turns resources into future assets.

04

Human Capital 也需要配置。Human capital also needs allocation.

健康、职业主线、AI、写作、网站和复盘系统，都属于人的资产表。Health, career line, AI, writing, websites, and review systems all belong on the human balance sheet.
